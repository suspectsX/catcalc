About CatCalc

CatCalc is a small website run by an independent creator based in China who loves cats and makes short cat videos (the "Pumpkin Cat" series). It offers free, quick tools that answer everyday cat-care questions, based on published veterinary guidelines (AAHA/AAFP, ASPCA).

The site also sells one digital product, the printable Cat Care Planner. There are no physical products and nothing is shipped.

We are not veterinarians. Nothing on this site replaces advice from your own vet.

Contact: tianxiemoyu@gmail.com

CatCalc provides general information only and is not a substitute for veterinary advice. If you think your cat ate something toxic, call your vet, the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center (888-426-4435, fee may apply) or Pet Poison Helpline (855-764-7661).
