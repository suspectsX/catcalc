About CatCalc

CatCalc is a small, independent site run by cat lovers who also make short cat videos. We build quick, free tools that answer everyday cat-care questions, based on published veterinary guidelines (AAHA/AAFP, ASPCA). We are not veterinarians, and nothing here replaces advice from your own vet.

CatCalc provides general information only and is not a substitute for veterinary advice. If you think your cat ate something toxic, call your vet, the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center (888-426-4435, fee may apply) or Pet Poison Helpline (855-764-7661).
