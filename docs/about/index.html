About CatCalc

CatCalc is a small English-language website run by an independent creator based in China who loves cats and publishes short cat videos (the “Pumpkin Cat” series). The site exists to answer everyday questions quickly: How old is my cat in human years? How much should I feed? Can cats eat this leftover? What name fits an orange kitten?

What we publish

  • Browser tools that run on your device—no account and no upload of the numbers you type.
  • Food-safety explainers for 41 human foods, with honest notes when cat-specific evidence is limited.
  • Original guides on topics such as lilies, reading food labels, new kittens, and senior weight changes.
  • A printable Cat Care Planner preview (Coming soon / not yet available)—not for sale yet, no shipping of physical goods.

How we handle accuracy

Calculator math and life-stage labels cite publicly available veterinary-organization resources (for example AAHA/AAFP life stage and nutrition materials). Food pages lean on ASPCA poison-control style guidance and clearly marked literature when a claim is unusual. We do not invent case studies, fake veterinary reviewers, income promises, or made-up “% of cats” statistics.

When evidence differs between dogs and cats, we say so. Read how ratings work for the full method.

Who we are not

We are not veterinarians, not a poison-control center, and not an emergency service. Nothing on this site replaces advice from your own vet. If your cat may have eaten something toxic, contact a clinic or hotline immediately—do not wait for an email reply from us.
