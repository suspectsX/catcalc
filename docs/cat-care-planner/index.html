About this planner

Your cat can't tell you when something's off, but a written record can. The Cat Care Planner (Pumpkin Cat Edition) is a 22-page printable PDF that keeps all of your cat's health and care information in one place, so vet visits are easier and small changes don't slip by.

The planned formats are US Letter and A4 for home printing. Preview pages are drafts, not a delivered product. Food-risk wording in the PDF previews still needs checking against the revised website before release.

What's inside

  • Cat Profile: microchip, breed, food, likes and quirks, with a photo spot
  • Emergency Contacts, with the US pet poison hotline numbers already printed
  • Vet Visit Log, plus a Vaccination & Flea/Worm Prevention Record
  • Weight Tracker, plus a 12-month Weight Chart grid
  • Weekly Daily Feeding Log and Water & Litter Box Log
  • Undated Monthly Calendar
  • Medication Tracker
  • Daily Health Check (14 quick checks, Mon–Sun)
  • "When to Call the Vet" warning-signs page
  • Grooming & Dental Log
  • New Food & Treat Trial Log
  • Monthly Expenses Tracker and Yearly Budget Summary
  • Draft food-risk reference (wording must be checked before release)
  • Plants & Household Hazards (lilies, medicines, essential oils and more)
  • Pet Sitter Info Sheet
  • Dotted Notes page
