Foods that are toxic to cats

Chocolate, Onions, Garlic, Chives, Grapes, Raisins, Alcohol, Coffee, Raw Bread Dough, Xylitol (Sugar-Free Gum & Sweets).

Also dangerous: lilies (true lilies and daylilies). Even pollen or vase water can cause kidney failure in cats, so keep them out of homes with cats.

CatCalc provides general information only and is not a substitute for veterinary advice. If you think your cat ate something toxic, call your vet, the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center (888-426-4435, fee may apply) or Pet Poison Helpline (855-764-7661).
