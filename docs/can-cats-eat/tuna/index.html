Can cats eat tuna?

Caution · Only rarely, tiny amounts

Only with caution. Tuna is not ideal for cats.

Why

Cats love tuna, but human canned tuna is not a complete diet and a tuna-heavy diet has been linked to nutritional problems. Tuna packed in brine is also salty.

How to serve it

Offer tuna in spring water only as an occasional treat, not a meal replacement.

Always check with your vet if your cat has health conditions, and see the ASPCA list of people foods to avoid.
