Can cats eat cucumber?

By CatCalc editorial team · Updated 2026-11-02 · Not veterinary advice

Small treat

Offer a small amount of peeled cucumber sliced thinly.

Why

Cucumber is mostly water and safe for cats in small amounts.

Cucumber is mostly water and novelty for cats that bother to try it. Peel if the skin is waxed; slice thin to reduce gulping. Pickled cucumber brings vinegar and salt; seasoned salads often include onion or garlic. Cucumber is not a hydration plan and not a diet food—complete cat food and fresh water remain the basics.

Plain flesh vs pickles and salads

Peeled, thinly sliced plain cucumber flesh is the only form we describe as a small optional treat. Pickles add salt and vinegar; mixed salads often include onion, garlic or dressing.

How it compares with other produce

Like many watery vegetables, cucumber is novelty more than nutrition for obligate carnivores. Blueberries or pumpkin have their own pages and still must stay inside treat calories.

When to skip it

Skip cucumber if your cat has ongoing digestive disease unless your vet says otherwise, and skip any preparation you would not describe as plain and unsalted.

How to serve it

Peel and slice thinly.

Treats and complete diets

If this food is appropriate at all, keep it inside the daily calorie budget. Use the calorie calculator and keep treats near or under about 10% of total kcal. Human food is not a balanced cat diet.
