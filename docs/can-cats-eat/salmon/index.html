Can cats eat cooked salmon?

Small treat · cooked

Offer only a small amount of plain cooked salmon with all bones removed.

Why

Cooked salmon provides protein and omega-3 fatty acids.

How to serve it

Cook plainly, remove all bones and keep portions small.

Sources and limits
