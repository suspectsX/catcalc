Can cats eat blueberries?

Yes · Safe in moderation

Yes, in moderation. Blueberries is generally safe for cats.

Why

Blueberries are non-toxic and full of antioxidants. Many cats ignore them, but they are a safe low-calorie treat.

How to serve it

Offer one or two, cut in half for small cats.

Always check with your vet if your cat has health conditions, and see the ASPCA list of people foods to avoid.
