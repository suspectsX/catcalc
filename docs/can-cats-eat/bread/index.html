Can cats eat bread?

By CatCalc editorial team · Updated 2026-11-02 · Not veterinary advice

Caution · plain baked only

Only a small piece of plain baked bread; avoid raw dough, garlic and raisin breads.

Why

Plain baked bread is not toxic, but it offers cats almost no nutrition. Garlic bread, raisin bread and raw dough are dangerous.

Plain baked bread is mostly starch and offers cats almost no useful nutrition. Garlic bread, onion focaccia, raisin bread and chocolate-chip loaves each import separate hazards. Raw yeast dough is an emergency topic on its own page. A crumb of plain bread is optional novelty, not a meal.

Plain baked vs flavored loaves

A small piece of plain baked bread is not typically toxic, but it is mostly starch with little feline nutrition. Garlic bread, onion focaccia, raisin bread and chocolate loaves each import separate hazards.

Raw dough is different

Unbaked yeast dough can expand and produce alcohol. That emergency is covered on the raw-dough page and should not be confused with a toast crumb.

How to serve a crumb if at all

Plain, fully baked, no spread. Skip butter garlic, seeds of uncertain type in quantity, and sweet fillings. Bread should never replace meat-based complete cat food.

How to serve it

A small piece of plain bread is fine occasionally.

Treats and complete diets

If this food is appropriate at all, keep it inside the daily calorie budget. Use the calorie calculator and keep treats near or under about 10% of total kcal. Human food is not a balanced cat diet.
