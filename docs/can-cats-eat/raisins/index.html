Can cats eat raisins?

No · Toxic / avoid

No. Raisins is not safe for cats.

Why

Raisins are dried grapes and carry the same kidney risk, in a more concentrated form. Watch out for raisin bread, trail mix and cereal.

What to do

Call your vet promptly.

Always check with your vet if your cat has health conditions, and see the ASPCA list of people foods to avoid.
