Can cats eat grapes?

No · Toxic / avoid

No. Grapes is not safe for cats.

Why

Grapes have been linked to sudden kidney injury in dogs, and the ASPCA lists grapes and raisins as toxic to cats too. The toxic dose is unknown, so no amount is considered safe.

What to do

Call your vet promptly; early treatment matters for kidney protection.

Always check with your vet if your cat has health conditions, and see the ASPCA list of people foods to avoid.
