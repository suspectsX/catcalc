Can cats eat catnip?

Yes · Safe in moderation

Yes, in moderation. Catnip is generally safe for cats.

Why

Catnip is non-toxic. About half to two-thirds of cats respond to it with playful behavior.

How to serve it

If a cat eats a lot, it may vomit or have diarrhea; offer in moderation.

Always check with your vet if your cat has health conditions, and see the ASPCA list of people foods to avoid.
