Can cats eat rice?

By CatCalc editorial team · Updated 2026-11-02 · Not veterinary advice

Small treat · cooked

Only a little plain cooked rice; it cannot replace a complete cat diet.

Why

Plain cooked white rice is easy to digest and sometimes recommended for short bland diets.

Plain cooked white rice is sometimes used short-term in bland diets under veterinary direction. It is easy to digest starch, not a protein substitute, and should not crowd out a complete cat diet. Fried rice, garlic rice and onion pilaf are different foods.

Plain cooked rice under guidance

Plain cooked white rice is sometimes used short-term in bland diets when a veterinarian recommends it. It is digestible starch, not a protein substitute.

What not to serve

Fried rice, garlic rice, onion pilaf and cheesy rice are human sides with hazards or excess fat. Stick to plain if rice is used at all.

After digestive upset

If vomiting or diarrhea continues, do not keep escalating home bland diets—recheck with your vet. Rice is a temporary tool, not a diagnosis.

How to serve it

Plain and cooked only; it should not replace meat.

Treats and complete diets

If this food is appropriate at all, keep it inside the daily calorie budget. Use the calorie calculator and keep treats near or under about 10% of total kcal. Human food is not a balanced cat diet.
