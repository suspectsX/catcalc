Can cats eat oatmeal?

By CatCalc editorial team · Updated 2026-11-02 · Not veterinary advice

Small treat · water-cooked

Offer a little plain oatmeal cooked with water, not milk or sweeteners.

Why

Plain cooked oats are safe and contain fiber.

Cook thoroughly with water. Sweeteners, milk and sugar-free syrups do not belong in the pot.

How to serve it

Cook with water (not milk) and serve a small amount.

Treats and complete diets

If this food is appropriate at all, keep it inside the daily calorie budget. Use the calorie calculator and keep treats near or under about 10% of total kcal. Human food is not a balanced cat diet.
