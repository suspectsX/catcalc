Can cats eat avocado?

Avoid · pit and fat

Avoid feeding avocado; the pit and fat pose risks, and cat-specific persin evidence is limited.

If eaten, contact your veterinarian for advice. In the US: ASPCA Poison Control: 888-426-4435 (fee may apply). Elsewhere, contact a local veterinary emergency service.

Why

Severe persin toxicity is best documented in other species; cat-specific evidence is limited. The pit can cause choking or obstruction, and the fatty flesh may cause digestive upset.

What to do

Avoid feeding it on purpose. Contact your vet after pit ingestion, a large amount, or any symptoms.
