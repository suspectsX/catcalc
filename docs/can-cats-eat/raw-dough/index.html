Can cats eat raw bread dough?

By CatCalc editorial team · Updated 2026-11-02 · Not veterinary advice

Do not feed

Raw yeast dough can expand and produce alcohol in the stomach.

If your cat already ate this

  1. Contact your veterinarian or an emergency clinic now—do not wait for symptoms.
  2. In the US you can also call ASPCA Animal Poison Control: 888-426-4435 or Pet Poison Helpline: 855-764-7661 (fees may apply).
  3. Have ready: approximate amount and time, product packaging or ingredients, your cat’s weight, and any symptoms.
  4. Do not induce vomiting or give home remedies unless a veterinarian specifically instructs you to.

Why

Raw yeast dough can expand in the stomach and the fermenting yeast produces alcohol. Both are dangerous for cats.

Expansion plus alcohol production is the dual hazard. Bread that is fully baked is a different question (see the bread page) and still offers little nutrition.

What to do

Treat as an emergency and call your vet.
