Can cats eat apples?

Caution · Only rarely, tiny amounts

Only with caution. Apples is not ideal for cats.

Why

Apple flesh is fine in small amounts, but the ASPCA notes that stems, leaves and seeds contain cyanogenic compounds.

How to serve it

Offer a small piece of peeled flesh with the core and seeds removed.

Always check with your vet if your cat has health conditions, and see the ASPCA list of people foods to avoid.
