Can cats eat peas?

By CatCalc editorial team · Updated 2026-11-02 · Not veterinary advice

Small treat · plain

Offer a small amount of plain cooked or thawed peas without seasoning.

Why

Peas are common in commercial cat food and safe plain.

Peas appear in many commercial cat-food recipes. Plain cooked or thawed frozen peas without seasoning can be a rare extra for cats that accept them. Butter, mint sauce, onion gravy and creamy casseroles turn peas into a different dish. Peas do not replace animal protein; keep any extras inside the treat calorie budget and a complete diet as the everyday staple.

Plain peas as a rare extra

Peas appear in many commercial cat foods. Plain cooked or thawed frozen peas without seasoning can be a rare extra for cats that eat them. Mash or split large peas for gulpers.

Seasoning traps

Butter, mint sauce, onion gravy and creamy casseroles turn peas into a different dish. Serve only what you can honestly call plain and unsalted.

Complete diet still wins

Peas do not replace animal protein. Keep any extras inside the treat calorie budget and use complete cat food as the everyday staple.

How to serve it

Cooked or thawed frozen peas, no seasoning.

Treats and complete diets

If this food is appropriate at all, keep it inside the daily calorie budget. Use the calorie calculator and keep treats near or under about 10% of total kcal. Human food is not a balanced cat diet.
