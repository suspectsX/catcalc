Can cats eat raw eggs?

By CatCalc editorial team · Updated 2026-11-02 · Not veterinary advice

Cook first

Do not serve raw eggs. Cook eggs fully without seasoning before sharing.

Why

Raw eggs can carry Salmonella or E. coli, and raw egg white contains avidin, which can interfere with biotin absorption.

Raw eggs can carry Salmonella or E. coli. Raw egg white also contains avidin, which can interfere with biotin absorption when fed often. Fully cooking eggs addresses the main household-sharing risks; adding butter, salt, onion powder or chili oil afterward reintroduces other problems.

Pathogens and avidin

Raw eggs may carry Salmonella or E. coli. Raw egg white contains avidin, which can interfere with biotin absorption when raw eggs are fed often. These are reasons CatCalc says cook first for household sharing.

After cooking, watch the seasonings

Scrambled café eggs often include butter, milk, cheese, salt, onion or chili oil. Those additions can reintroduce problems covered on other pages. Plain is the only template we describe.

Compared with cooked eggs

Fully cooked eggs can be a small protein treat for some cats. Raw eggs are the preparation to avoid for casual sharing; see the cooked-eggs page for portion caveats.

Cook before serving

Cook eggs fully (scrambled or boiled, no seasoning).
