Can cats eat raw eggs?

Cook first

Do not serve raw eggs. Cook eggs fully without seasoning before sharing.

Why

Raw eggs can carry Salmonella or E. coli, and raw egg white contains avidin, which can interfere with biotin absorption.

Cook before serving

Cook eggs fully (scrambled or boiled, no seasoning).
