Can cats eat milk?

Caution · lactose

Cow's milk often causes digestive upset; it is not a kitten milk replacement.

Why

Most adult cats become lactose intolerant after weaning, so cow's milk often causes upset stomach and diarrhea. Kittens should get kitten milk replacer, never cow's milk.

Milk alternatives

If you want to offer milk, use a lactose-free 'cat milk' as an occasional treat.
