Can cats eat garlic?

Do not feed

Garlic can damage red blood cells; avoid it in all forms.

If eaten, contact your veterinarian for advice. In the US: ASPCA Poison Control: 888-426-4435 (fee may apply). Elsewhere, contact a local veterinary emergency service.

Why

Garlic is an Allium like onions and is considered even more potent. Garlic powder in broths, baby food and leftovers is a common hidden source.

Garlic salt, garlic oil and savory broth concentrates are common hidden sources. Treat any Allium exposure as something to discuss with a vet, not a wait-and-see experiment.

What to do

Contact your vet, and check labels of any human food or broth you share with your cat.

How we rate this food

Rating focus: prevent exposure and get professional advice after ingestion. We do not provide home treatment protocols for garlic.

Labels follow the method in How CatCalc rates human foods. Dog-toxicity claims are not copied onto cats without an evidence note.

Treats, calories and complete diets

Even generally safer foods must fit inside your cat’s daily calorie budget. Use the calorie calculator and keep treats near or under about 10% of total kcal. Human food is not a balanced cat diet.
