Can cats eat pumpkin?

Small treat · plain

Offer a small amount of plain pumpkin, not sweetened or spiced pie filling.

Why

Plain cooked or canned pumpkin contains fiber; it is not a treatment for an unexplained digestive problem. Pie filling may contain sugar, spices or other unsuitable ingredients.

How to serve it

Offer only a small amount of plain pumpkin. Ask your vet about ongoing constipation or diarrhea.
