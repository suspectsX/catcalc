Can cats eat strawberries?

By CatCalc editorial team · Updated 2026-11-02 · Not veterinary advice

Small treat

Offer only a small strawberry piece with the leaves removed.

Why

Strawberries are non-toxic for cats, though sugary.

Remove leafy tops. Sugary fruit is optional; complete cat food remains the staple.

How to serve it

Offer a small piece with the leaves removed.

Treats and complete diets

If this food is appropriate at all, keep it inside the daily calorie budget. Use the calorie calculator and keep treats near or under about 10% of total kcal. Human food is not a balanced cat diet.
