Can cats eat peanut butter?

By CatCalc editorial team · Updated 2026-11-02 · Not veterinary advice

Caution · ingredients

At most a lick of xylitol-free peanut butter; it is sticky and high in fat.

Why

Peanut butter is not toxic, but it is sticky, high in fat and calories, and some brands contain xylitol.

Stickiness plus calories plus possible xylitol is a poor combination. A smear on a toy for enrichment is still optional, not required.

Check ingredients first

Check the label for xylitol and offer no more than a lick.

Treats and complete diets

If this food is appropriate at all, keep it inside the daily calorie budget. Use the calorie calculator and keep treats near or under about 10% of total kcal. Human food is not a balanced cat diet.
