Can cats eat cooked shrimp?

Small treat · cooked

Plain cooked shrimp can be a small treat with the shell and tail removed.

Why

Plain cooked shrimp is a tasty, high-protein treat most cats love.

Shells and tails are choking hazards. Garlic butter restaurant shrimp is not the same as plain boiled shrimp.

How to serve it

Remove the shell, tail and digestive tract; no garlic butter.

How we rate this food

Rating focus: Cooked Shrimp can be a small plain treat for many healthy cats. It is still optional. Complete cat food remains the staple diet.

Labels follow the method in How CatCalc rates human foods. Dog-toxicity claims are not copied onto cats without an evidence note.

Treats, calories and complete diets

Even generally safer foods must fit inside your cat’s daily calorie budget. Use the calorie calculator and keep treats near or under about 10% of total kcal. Human food is not a balanced cat diet.
