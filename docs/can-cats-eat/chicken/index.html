Can cats eat cooked chicken?

Yes · Safe in moderation

Yes, in moderation. Cooked Chicken is generally safe for cats.

Why

Plain cooked chicken is lean protein that cats naturally thrive on. It is one of the best human foods to share.

How to serve it

Serve boneless, skinless and unseasoned (no garlic, onion or salt).

Always check with your vet if your cat has health conditions, and see the ASPCA list of people foods to avoid.
