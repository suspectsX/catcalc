Can cats eat chives?

No · Toxic / avoid

No. Chives is not safe for cats.

Why

Chives, leeks and scallions are Allium plants and can damage red blood cells in cats.

What to do

Contact your vet if your cat ate a noticeable amount.

Always check with your vet if your cat has health conditions, and see the ASPCA list of people foods to avoid.
