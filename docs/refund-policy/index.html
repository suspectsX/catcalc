Our products are digital downloads, so we can't take them back once they are delivered. We still want you to be happy with your purchase.

When you can get a refund

Within 14 days of purchase, you can ask for a full refund if:

How to request one

Email tianxiemoyu@gmail.com with your order number and a short description of the problem, plus a screenshot if you can. We usually reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through our payment provider.
