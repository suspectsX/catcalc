The formula

Vets start from the Resting Energy Requirement: RER = 70 × (body weight in kg)0.75. This is multiplied by a factor for life stage and activity, for example 1.2 for a neutered adult or 0.8 for weight loss. These are starting points; adjust based on your cat's body condition and ask your vet, especially for weight loss, pregnancy or illness.

Source: AAHA nutrition and weight management guidelines. The estimate is a starting point, not an individual feeding prescription. Treats are part of the total budget.

FAQ

How many calories does a cat need per day?

A typical 4.5 kg (10 lb) neutered indoor adult cat needs roughly 215-260 kcal per day (inactive to neutered-adult factor), but needs vary. The calculator uses RER = 70 x (weight in kg)^0.75 multiplied by a life-stage factor.

How much wet or dry food should I feed my cat?

Divide your cat's daily calorie needs by the calories in the food (printed on the bag or can as kcal/kg, kcal/cup or kcal/can).

How many treats can a cat have?

Keep treats under about 10% of the total daily calorie budget. Subtract treat calories from the food allowance rather than adding them on top.
