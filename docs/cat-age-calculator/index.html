How the calculation works

Cats mature very fast: the first year equals about 15 human years and the second year adds about 9 more. After age 2, each cat year equals roughly 4 human years. Ages under 1 year are approximate and based on typical kitten development.

FAQ

How old is my cat in human years?

This informal approximation uses about 15 human years at age 1, 24 at age 2, then 4 more per cat year. It is not a clinical measurement or an endorsed AAHA/AAFP formula.

What are the life stages of a cat?

Kitten: birth to 1 year. Young adult: 1-6 years. Mature adult: 7-10 years. Senior: over 10 years (2021 AAHA/AAFP Feline Life Stage Guidelines).

How long do indoor cats live?

Many indoor cats live into their mid-teens, and some reach 20 or more. Regular vet checks become more important from the senior stage.

Life stage definitions: 2021 AAHA/AAFP guidelines. These definitions are separate from the informal human-year estimate above.
